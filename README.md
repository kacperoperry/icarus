# Icarus

A browser game based on the Greek myth of Icarus and Daedalus.

Help Icarus fly island by island from Crete to Sicily. Stay between the sun and
the sea: fly too high and the wax on his wings melts, fly too low and the waves
soak his feathers.

## How to play

- **↑ or Space**: beat wings (fly up)
- **↓**: dive
- On a phone, use the **Rise** and **Dive** buttons.

Follow Daedalus's glowing trail. Collect Athena's shields (+10 points each). A
shield saves you once, and you have 3 lives.

## What you'll face

Gulls and their droppings, leaping and flying fish, cloud cover, tides, hot and
cold winds, Charybdis's whirlpool, Thera's volcano, two storms, and Scylla's
six heads guarding the strait before Sicily.
