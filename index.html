Island by island to Sicily

Keep Icarus between the sun and the sea, from Crete to Sicily. Gulls drop droppings that weigh down his wings. Fish leap and glide. Athena's shields save him once from anything, and he has 3 lives. The weather shifts the safe zone up and down. Thera's volcano blows ash and fire, Charybdis churns the whirlpool strait, and Scylla waits in the last one. Two storms soak his feathers, but warm air dries them. Follow Daedalus's trail.
